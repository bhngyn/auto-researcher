Summary of findings

  • The Valley Courier reported that a tank at the Ridgeline Chemical plant at Millbrook released an estimated 40 cubic metres of solvent into the Foo River on 3 March 2024.{{c:r1-upper-basin-001}} Ridgeline Chemical said the release was a fraction of the volume alleged.{{c:r1-upper-basin-003}}
  • The Regional Water Authority reported that discharge from the Millbrook outfall exceeded the permitted limit on 10 February 2025.{{c:r2-permit-001}}
  • The Foo Valley Municipal Utility confirmed a chlorine overdose at the Lower Basin treatment works on 14 July 2025.{{c:r1-lower-basin-001}} The Regional Health Office recorded 48 cases of skin irritation at Riverside Clinic between 14 and 17 July 2025, and does not link them to the overdose.{{c:r1-lower-basin-002}}
