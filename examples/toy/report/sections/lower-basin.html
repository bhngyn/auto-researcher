Lower Basin

Three events are recorded in the Lower Basin.

Water main burst in Eastfield (20 November 2024)

20–23 November 2024 · Eastfield · Foo Valley Municipal Utility · Grade A{{c:r2-mainburst-001}}

The Foo Valley Municipal Utility said a water main burst in Eastfield on 20 November 2024 and that it issued a boil notice for 3,400 customers until 23 November 2024.{{c:r2-mainburst-001}}

Chlorine overdose at the Lower Basin treatment works (14 July 2025)

14 July 2025 · Lower Basin treatment works · Foo Valley Municipal Utility · Grade B{{c:r1-lower-basin-001}}

The Foo Valley Municipal Utility confirmed that on 14 July 2025 the Lower Basin treatment works dosed chlorine at four times the permitted level for roughly three hours.{{c:r1-lower-basin-001}}

Skin irritation cluster at Riverside Clinic (Eastfield, 14–17 July 2025)

14–17 July 2025 · Riverside Clinic, Eastfield · not attributed by the source · Grade B{{c:r1-lower-basin-002}}

The Regional Health Office recorded 48 cases of skin irritation at Riverside Clinic between 14 and 17 July 2025.{{c:r1-lower-basin-002}}
