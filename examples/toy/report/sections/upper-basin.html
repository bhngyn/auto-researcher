Upper Basin

Three events are recorded in the Upper Basin between 2024 and 2025.

Solvent release at the Millbrook plant (Millbrook, 3 March 2024)

3 March 2024 · Millbrook · Ridgeline Chemical, per the Valley Courier · Grade A{{c:r1-upper-basin-001}}

The Valley Courier reported that a tank at the Ridgeline Chemical plant at Millbrook released an estimated 40 cubic metres of solvent into the Foo River on 3 March 2024.{{c:r1-upper-basin-001}}

Ridgeline Chemical said the release was a fraction of the volume alleged and that no fish kill could be attributed to the plant.{{c:r1-upper-basin-003}}

Fish kill below Millbrook (Foo River, 5 March 2024)

5 March 2024 · Foo River below Millbrook · not attributed by the source · Grade B{{c:r1-upper-basin-002}}

Basin Watch reported that its volunteers counted about 2,300 dead fish along a 9 km stretch of the Foo River below Millbrook on 5 March 2024.{{c:r1-upper-basin-002}}

Discharge above the permitted limit at the Millbrook outfall (10 February 2025)

10 February 2025 · Millbrook outfall · Ridgeline Chemical, per the Regional Water Authority · Grade B{{c:r2-permit-001}}

The Regional Water Authority reported that sampling on 10 February 2025 found solvent discharged from the Millbrook outfall exceeded the permitted limit by a factor of two, and that it issued a compliance order to Ridgeline Chemical.{{c:r2-permit-001}}
