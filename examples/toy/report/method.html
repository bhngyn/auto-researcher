Scope, sources and method

Scope

This is a synthetic example. It records what invented open sources document about contamination events in an invented river basin between 1 January 2024 and 31 December 2025. The conduct of the Regional Water Authority appears only as context, and its absence as a subject is not a finding.

Sources

The evidence base consists of {{STAT:USABLE}} usable claims supported by {{STAT:URLS}} distinct web pages. {{STAT:PARTISAN}} source citations are from a party to the matter and are presented as claims by that party.

Evidence grades

Grade A: {{STAT:GA}} claims. Grade B: {{STAT:GB}} claims. Grade C: {{STAT:GC}} claims. Of {{STAT:TOTAL}} claims recorded, {{STAT:REJECTED}} were rejected because no reachable source supported them.

Verification

Every claim went through a research pass, a mechanical excerpt check against the live page, and a separate sceptical fact-check. {{STAT:CORRECTED}} were corrected or narrowed.

Limitations

A record that rests only on grade-C evidence is kept in the database, marked as weakly sourced, and is not given a card in the text.
